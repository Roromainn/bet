CALCULATION FORMULAS IMPLEMENTED
==============================================================================

1. LAY BET EV (Expected Value):
   Formula: EV = back_stake * (back_odds - 1) - lay_stake * lay_odds

   Example:
   - Back 100€ at 3.0 odds on Team A
   - Lay 150€ at 1.5 odds on Team A (opposite)
   - EV = 100 * (3.0 - 1) - 150 * 1.5 = 200 - 225 = -25€

2. MATCHED BET PROFIT:
   Guaranteed profit when both outcomes are covered
   Formula: Profit = min(stake1 * odds1, stake2 * odds2) - total_stakes

3. COVERAGE LOSS:
   Amount lost due to imperfect matching
   Formula: Loss = total_stakes - min(outcome1, outcome2)
